## What We Will Demonstrate

1. One Copilot-style entry point receives a synthetic Security Review request.
2. A deterministic Router executes five pinned Skills through four reusable Plugins.
3. The user inspects evidence, versions, policy outcomes, and the ordered trace.
4. An analyst confirms or changes the proposed risk decision.
5. ESP generates a cited draft report and Evaluation Run result.
6. A second Consumer reuses an existing Skill without copying its assets.

## Responsible AI and Security

- synthetic, non-sensitive demo data only;
- explicit separation of facts, model suggestions, tool results, rules, and human decisions;
- no unsupported material claim without evidence;
- no silent authorization bypass or identity switching;
- prompt injection in source content cannot override workflow policy;
- final risk requires human confirmation;
- no autonomous approval, rejection, consent, remediation, or business-system write;
- Demo/Mock integrations are visibly labelled and cannot qualify for Pilot.

## Business Value

- reduce duplicated prompts, workflows, integrations, and evaluation logic;
- accelerate delivery by reusing governed capabilities across Copilots;
- improve trust through evidence, ownership, versioning, and human accountability;
- reduce change risk through compatibility and impact analysis;
- measure capability reuse, quality, governance coverage, maintainability, cost, and business impact.
